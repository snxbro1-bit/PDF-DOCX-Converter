PDF to DOCX FAQ

Is this PDF to DOCX converter free?

Yes. You can use this tool without paying for a conversion service.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

The conversion processing is designed to happen in your browser. Your PDF content is not intentionally uploaded to our own server.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Yes, the tool can attempt OCR processing for scanned or image-based PDF pages. OCR results can vary depending on document quality, fonts and language.

Will the DOCX look exactly like the original PDF?

Not always. PDF and DOCX use different document structures. Text extraction and basic document structure can be recreated, but complicated layouts, tables, images and special formatting may change.

Can I edit the converted DOCX?

Yes. The generated file is a DOCX Word document that can be opened and edited using Microsoft Word and compatible office applications.
